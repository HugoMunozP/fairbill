What to know about AT&T Fiber pricing

AT&T simplified its fiber lineup in June 2026. Fiber plans have symmetrical upload and download speeds, and bundling with AT&T wireless lowers the price.

Expect your bill to rise once the introductory price ends. By our estimate, AT&T Fiber plans can cost up to about $8 more per month at the regular rate. The exact increase depends on your plan and address.

How to lower your AT&T Fiber bill

  1. Find your exact monthly price for internet only on your latest bill.
  2. Compare it with the advertised price above and with what neighbors pay on Fairbill.
  3. Call AT&T Fiber and say: "Your website lists this plan at $55 for new customers. I'd like a better price, or I'll look at other providers."
  4. If the first agent can't help, ask for the retention (cancellations) department.
