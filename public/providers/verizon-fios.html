What to know about Verizon Fios pricing

Verizon Fios prices include a multi-year price guarantee on eligible plans and are lower if you also have a Verizon phone plan.

Expect your bill to rise once the introductory price ends. By our estimate, Verizon Fios plans can cost up to about $9 more per month at the regular rate. The exact increase depends on your plan and address.

How to lower your Verizon Fios bill

  1. Find your exact monthly price for internet only on your latest bill.
  2. Compare it with the advertised price above and with what neighbors pay on Fairbill.
  3. Call Verizon Fios and say: "Your website lists this plan at $50 for new customers. I'd like a better price, or I'll look at other providers."
  4. If the first agent can't help, ask for the retention (cancellations) department.
