What to know about Xfinity pricing

Xfinity has offered new customers multi-year price guarantees, but customers on older plans often pay far more than the current new-customer price for the same speed.

Expect your bill to rise once the introductory price ends. By our estimate, Xfinity plans can cost up to about $60 more per month at the regular rate. The exact increase depends on your plan and address.

How to lower your Xfinity bill

  1. Find your exact monthly price for internet only on your latest bill.
  2. Compare it with the advertised price above and with what neighbors pay on Fairbill.
  3. Call Xfinity and say: "Your website lists this plan at $40 for new customers. I'd like a better price, or I'll look at other providers."
  4. If the first agent can't help, ask for the retention (cancellations) department.
