Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you share a bill we store your ZIP code, internet provider, plan, monthly price, how long you've been a customer, the date, and a random ID kept in your browser so the same device isn't counted twice. We do not ask for your name, email, street address or account number.

Bill photos

If you choose to upload a photo of your bill, it is stored privately. A person checks that the price matches what you reported, and the photo is deleted within 7 days. Please cover your name, address and account number before uploading.

How we use the data

We publish only summaries, such as the typical price for a plan in a ZIP code, and only once at least 5 people have reported that plan. Individual prices are shown as anonymous dots with no other details. We may share aggregated, anonymous price statistics with researchers or businesses.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Your choices

Because we don't collect who you are, we can't link a report back to you. If you want a report removed, contact us with the ZIP, provider, price and the date you shared it.
