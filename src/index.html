Questions

  
Is Fairbill free?
Yes. The site is supported by ads and by provider offers, which are always labeled. Providers can never pay to change the prices people report.

  
Why do my neighbors pay less for the same plan?
Usually because they signed up recently or called to negotiate. Introductory prices often jump after 12 to 24 months, and nothing on your bill tells you others are paying less.

  
How do you stop fake reports?
We show the median, so a few odd prices barely move the result. Prices far outside the normal range for a plan aren't counted. Each device counts once per plan every six months. Reports verified with a bill photo count double, and we only show prices once a plan has at least 5 reports in a ZIP.

  
What is the "estimate" I see in some ZIPs?
Until 5 neighbors report a plan, we show a range based on the provider's advertised price and its usual price after the promo ends. It's clearly labeled and disappears once real reports arrive.

  
Why do I have to share before I see results?
Because the data only exists if people share. If you don't know your exact price, you can still see the typical price for your plan.
